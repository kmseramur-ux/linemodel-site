A play needs a 3-point gap between the model and the market, and the model's side must not be the more injured team or coming off a 17-point loss. Leans are 2 to 3 points. Market is the consensus line; Kalshi is the live line implied by its spread ladder. Best price is the highest expected value for the side across every number Kalshi lists within 3 points of the market, fees included, if the model's margin is right. The Season view shows how often it has been.

Model

what it is and how it has tested

Power ratings from free play-by-play: Elo, opponent-adjusted EPA and success rate, the starting quarterback's prior efficiency, rest and situation. A tree model and a ridge regression are averaged. Every number for a game uses only games finished before that week. The backtest trains on every prior season and predicts the next, 2015 to 2026, graded against the closing spread.
